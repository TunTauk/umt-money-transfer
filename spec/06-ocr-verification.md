# Manual Entry; OCR Out of Scope

Screenshot upload, attachment storage, OCR extraction, provider-specific OCR,
and screenshot verification are out of scope.

Cash In and Cash Out forms do not accept an external reference or manual
date/time. They do not include a screenshot control, OCR indicator,
verification checkbox, or any OCR-derived value. The system generates the
record reference and posting timestamp. Creation is a single immediate posting
action governed by the amount and account rules in
[Transactions & Lifecycle](04-transactions-lifecycle.md).
