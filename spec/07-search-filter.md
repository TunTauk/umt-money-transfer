# Search & Filter

Cash In and Cash Out lists provide free-text search across customer name,
normalized customer phone, compact ID, system reference, and note.

Available structured filters are:

- Date range
- Account type and selected account
- Created by, Owner only
- Amount range

There is no status filter. Lists are type-specific and default to newest first.
Teller queries are always permission-scoped to records involving the Teller's
active assigned accounts, including records created by another staff member on
a shared account. Owner queries may cover all records.
